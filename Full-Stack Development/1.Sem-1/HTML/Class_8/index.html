Concept — Landmark semantic tags.

In class 07 you learned that semantic tags describe their
meaning to the browser and the developer, unlike generic <div> containers. HTML gives us a set
of "landmark" semantic tags specifically meant for page layout:
<header> — introductory content, usually a logo/title and navigation
<nav> — a block of navigation links
<main> — the primary, unique content of the page (only one per page)
<section> — a thematic grouping of content, usually with its own heading
<article> — a self-contained piece of content that could stand on its own (a blog post, a news
story, a product card)
<aside> — content indirectly related to the main content (a sidebar, a related-links box, an ad
<footer> — closing content, usually copyright, contact info, or links
